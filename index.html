Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To enhance the professions of management by preparing students and scholars in management sciences, computer sciences, and languages capable of competition and meeting stakeholder expectations through exceptional educational and training programs that link theory with practice.

Our Vision

To be a distinguished educational organization in Egypt and the Arab world, recognized for excellence in academics, research, and student development, producing graduates who are leaders and innovators in their respective fields.

Vision, Mission & Goals

Vision

The Faculty of Computers and Information at Sadat Academy for Management Sciences aspires to advance the scientific, applied, and research level in Computer Science, Information Systems, and Software Engineering — achieving a distinguished standing among FCI faculties locally, regionally, and globally — while upholding professional ethics, developing Egypt's information society, and managing its information resources and heritage, through qualifying outstanding human capital that contributes to community development.

Mission

The faculty strives to provide a distinguished educational and research level in Computer Science, Information Systems, and Software Engineering for its graduates, preparing them academically and professionally to meet society's evolving aspirations and push them to build the information-technology infrastructure of organizations, with attention to diversity in scientific research and development, and providing an environment that encourages creativity, excellence, and professional ethical commitment.

Strategic Goals

  1. Prepare Specialists: Equip graduates with theoretical and applied foundations in computing and information technology, qualifying them to compete in the global market.
  2. Conduct Research: Perform scientific and applied studies directly impacting information resources and community development.
  3. Technical Consultation: Provide scientific and technical consultations to organizations utilizing computing and information technology.
  4. Train Technical Staff: Train professionals in various state sectors in computing technology and information management.
  5. Raise Awareness: Spread understanding of computing technology and rational information resource management across sectors.
  6. Organize Scientific Events: Conduct conferences and scientific meetings to elevate educational levels.
  7. Build International Partnerships: Conclude scientific agreements with equivalent institutions locally and internationally.
  8. Support Research Publishing: Provide and support scientific publishing and research means.
  9. Establish Research Units: Create advanced specialized research units in various CS and information management branches.

Academic Departments

All instruction is conducted in English. The Faculty of Computers and Information offers five distinct specializations at the Bachelor's degree level:

1. Department of Computer Sciences

Computer Science covers a wide range of theoretical foundations contributing to robotics, computer vision, intelligent systems, bioinformatics, and more. It solves complex problems using mathematical analysis, algorithms, programming languages, and system/network security.

Key Topics Include:

Algorithm Analysis & Design • Computer Architecture • Computer Programming • Programming Language Concepts • Object-Oriented Programming • Web Application Development • Data Structures • Operating Systems • Computer Networks • Artificial Intelligence • Machine Learning • Computer Security • Cloud & Mobile Computing • Computer Vision • Pattern Recognition • Virtual Reality • Graduation Project

2. Department of Information Systems

Information Systems focuses on developing, operating, and maintaining systems that enable information and communication services in modern organizations. It bridges Computer Science and the business world by understanding organizational problems and providing technical solutions.

Key Topics Include:

IS Fundamentals • IS Analysis & Design • IS Development Methodologies • E-Business Strategy & Architecture • Expert Systems • Knowledge-Based Systems • Database Systems • E-Commerce • Decision Support Systems • IS Project Management • IS Quality Assurance • Graduation Project

3. Department of Software Engineering

Software Engineering relates to developing software systems efficiently, reliably, and at scale, with ongoing maintenance. It covers designing and developing the foundations for building complete systems that meet user requirements.

Key Topics Include:

SE Fundamentals • Software Requirements • Software Design • Software Architecture • Software Testing • Software Maintenance • SE Project Management • Software Quality Management • SE Tools & Methods • Graduation Project

4. Artificial Intelligence

Trains students to build intelligent systems across the full AI stack — from automated reasoning and cognitive science through deep learning, NLP, robotics, and autonomous agents. Graduates pursue roles as AI/ML Engineers, NLP Engineers, Robotics Engineers, and Research Scientists.

5. Cyber Security

Prepares students to defend systems and networks against the full spectrum of digital threats. The curriculum spans cryptographic theory, penetration testing, digital forensics, risk management, cloud security, and cyber warfare. Graduates find roles as Penetration Testers, SOC Analysts, Digital Forensics Investigators, and Security Architects.

Specialization Rules:
• Choose a major and a minor from the five specializations (starting Year 3)
• The two cannot be in the same field
• Dual-specialization Bachelor's degrees are available
• Students may change specialization once during their study period

About Sadat Academy

Sadat Academy for Management Sciences (SAMS) is Egypt's premier educational organization dedicated to excellence in management sciences, computer sciences, and languages. We are committed to producing graduates who are capable of competing globally and meeting the expectations of stakeholders through exceptional educational and training programs that bridge theory with practice.

Our Historical Background

The roots of Sadat Academy trace back to 1954 with the establishment of the Public Administration training center, followed by the National center for Management Development in 1961 and the Local Administration training center in 1967. Sadat Academy for Management Sciences was formally established in March 1981 under Presidential Decree No. 172/1981 as an independent educational organization, becoming Egypt's first governmental body specialized in management sciences.

For over four decades, we have been a beacon of academic excellence and innovation in the region, serving thousands of students and professionals.

Admission Requirements & Process

Enrollment Requirements

You must:
✓ Hold a General Secondary Education certificate (Mathematics or Science stream) or equivalent
✓ Achieve at least 80% in English language
✓ Pass aptitude tests held by the faculty
✓ Successfully complete the personal interview

Transfers from Other Faculties

From Equivalent Faculties:

  • Must be passing at current faculty
  • Secondary school total must meet the Academy's minimum for your certificate year

From Non-Equivalent Faculties:

  • Must hold a secondary certificate total qualifying for FCI admission

General Requirements:

  • Must not have discontinued studies before transfer
  • Must not have been permanently dismissed for disciplinary reasons

Graduation Project

Fourth-year students must prepare a graduation project spanning two semesters (Semesters 7 & 8). The project is assessed both practically and orally by a faculty committee. Students may request a Faculty Council extension of up to 4 weeks after written exams to complete the project.

Additional Improvement Opportunity:
Upon completing all required graduation hours, students may register up to 3 additional elective courses to improve their cumulative GPA.

Academic Advising

The faculty assigns academic advisors from the teaching staff to guide students in choosing required courses before each registration period. Your advisor will help you make course selections aligned with your academic plan.

Registration Timeline & Process

Standard Registration Window

Registration opens: 1 week before semester start
Late registration available: First and second weeks after study begins
Requirement: Payment of tuition + approval from academic advisor and Vice Dean

Credit Load Requirements

Semester Type Minimum Maximum Special Cases
Regular Semester 15 hrs 21 hrs —

Add/Drop Courses

During the Academic Advising Period (1 week before + 2 weeks after semester start):

  • May add or drop up to 2 courses
  • No record on transcript
  • Requires academic advisor approval

After Advising Period (Until Midterm Exams):

  • Withdrawal recorded as "W" on transcript
  • Still requires advisor approval

After Midterm Exams:

  • ❌ No further withdrawals allowed

Registration Suspension (Enrollment Freeze)

Students may request to suspend registration for a semester under the following conditions:

  • Submit compelling reason within 8 weeks of semester start
  • Obtain Faculty Council approval
  • All previously earned grades are preserved

Suspension Limits

Maximum suspension:

  • 2 consecutive semesters, OR
  • 4 non-consecutive semesters throughout entire study period
Important: Suspension periods are NOT counted toward the time allowed to earn the Bachelor's degree.

GPA Calculation

Annual GPA: Weighted average of grades across both semesters of the year

Formula: Sum of (credit hours × grade points) ÷ Total credit hours

Cumulative GPA: Weighted average of all grades earned throughout the study period

GPA Range Symbol Grade

Honors Distinction

Honors distinction is awarded at graduation to students meeting ALL of the following criteria:

  • Cumulative GPA of 3.7 or higher
  • Studied at least 75% of graduation requirements at FCI
  • No failures in any course taken at FCI
  • Completed graduation requirements in 4 years or less (excluding suspension periods)

Incomplete Grades

A student receives an Incomplete (I) grade if they have:

  • A serious legitimate reason accepted by the Faculty Council
  • Absences within the allowed percentage
  • Valid excuse from final exam

Resolution: The Incomplete grade must be resolved before the next semester starts, coordinated with the course instructor by the Vice Dean.

Re-taking Failed Courses

If you fail a mandatory course, you must re-register and re-take it. Both the original fail grade and the new grade are recorded on your transcript.

Academic Policies & Regulations

Attendance & Absence Policy

⚠️ THE 25% RULE (CRITICAL)

If your absence exceeds 25% of total teaching hours for ANY course:
❌ You are FAILED in that course
❌ You are BARRED from the final exam

Exception: If your absence is excused and approved by Faculty Council, you receive a "W" (Withdrawn) grade instead.

Students must regularly attend:

  • Lectures and practical sessions
  • Midterm exams and coursework
  • Research labs and classrooms
  • All registered course activities

Important: Report absences to Student Affairs immediately.

National/Patriotic Duty Exemptions

Students assigned national or patriotic duties receive special consideration:

  • Absence for national duties is excused and doesn't count toward the 25% limit
  • Must complete coursework in alternative manner agreed with instructor
  • If missing midterm: take alternative exam (70%, 3 hours) at semester end
  • If missing final: may request Incomplete (I) grade

Academic Warning System

When You Get an Academic Warning:
Your cumulative GPA has fallen below 2.0

Actions Required:

  • Your maximum credit load is reduced to 15 hours per semester
  • You have 2 semesters to raise your cumulative GPA to at least 2.0
  • If you reach 2.0, the warning is cleared
  • If you don't reach 2.0 within 2 semesters, you may only re-register courses you previously failed
  • For elective courses, you may substitute with alternative courses
  • You have exactly 2 chances total to clear the warning

Specialization Change Option: You may transfer to another major specialization within the faculty if it improves your chances of clearing the warning (with Vice Dean approval).

Failure & Incomplete Procedures

Automatic Failure

If you don't sit for final exams, you are automatically FAILED in those courses.

Incomplete Grade (I)

If you have a serious legitimate reason approved by Faculty Council and your absence is within allowed limits, you may receive an Incomplete (I) grade instead of failure. You must resolve the Incomplete before the next semester starts.

Student Discipline System

The Faculty of Computers and Information follows the provisions of Universities Organization Law No. 49 of 1972 and its executive regulations regarding student discipline.

Disciplinary Violations

The following constitute disciplinary violations:

  • Actions disrupting faculty or university order
  • Obstructing studies, inciting others to skip classes, or organized refusal to attend lectures
  • Any act contrary to honor, dignity, or good conduct inside or outside the university
  • Disrupting exam order, cheating or attempting to cheat ⚠️
  • Damaging or wasting university facilities, equipment, materials, or books
  • Organizing unauthorized associations within the university
  • Distributing flyers or posting wall newspapers without prior permission
  • Sit-ins or demonstrations violating public order

Cheating Policy (Severe)

🚨 CONSEQUENCES OF CHEATING:

If caught cheating:
1. ✋ Immediately removed from exam room by Dean or representative
2. ❌ Barred from remaining exams of that session
3. ❌ Considered FAILED in ALL courses of that exam session
4. 📋 Case referred to Disciplinary Council for further action

Disciplinary Penalties

Penalties are applied in order of severity:

Penalty Level Type Details
1 Oral Warning Not recorded in file
2 Written Warning Recorded; guardian notified
3 Denial of Student Services Withdrawal of specific services
4 Course Attendance Ban Barred from specific course(s) up to 1 month
5 Faculty Suspension Temporary suspension from faculty up to 1 month
6 Exam Ban Barred from one or more exam(s)
7 Exam Cancellation Exam cancelled for one or more courses
8 Permanent Expulsion Communicated to all Egyptian universities; bars enrollment/exams nationwide

Recording & Notification

All disciplinary decisions except oral warnings are:

  • Kept permanently in the student's official file
  • Reported to the student's guardian
  • Referenced in future academic matters
